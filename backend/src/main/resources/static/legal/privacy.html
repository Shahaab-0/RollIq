Privacy Policy

RollIQ ("we", "the app") is a training log for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu practitioners. This page explains what data we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address and a hashed password. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Training data you enter: sessions, techniques and any reference links you attach to them, rolls/sparring logs (including partner names and submissions), belt promotions, and competition results.
  • Injury logs: if you choose to log an injury, that entry (body part, severity, notes) is stored the same way as your other training data — it is not treated as protected medical information and is not shared with anyone outside the app.
  • Gym data: if you join or create a gym in the app, your display name, role, and class attendance become visible to that gym's other members and trainers — that visibility is the point of the feature, not incidental.
  • Push notification token: if you enable notifications, we store a device token (from Apple/Google) so we can send you streak reminders and class updates. You can disable this at any time from your device's notification settings.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not show ads or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not use your training data for anything other than showing it back to you (and, for gym features, the other members of gyms you've joined).

Where your data lives

Your data is stored on a server we operate and is transmitted over an encrypted connection. It is retained for as long as your account exists.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete any training entry, roll, technique, injury, or competition at any time from within the app.
  • You can delete your account entirely from Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and everything tied to it — this cannot be undone.
  • You can turn off push notifications from your device settings at any time.

Children's privacy

RollIQ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
